# Telecom Churn & Retention Knowledge Base

> **Version:** 4 (generated by `src/kb_build.py`, not hand-written)
> **Purpose:** grounding context for the narration layer. It is READING MATERIAL
> for an LLM, not a decision engine.
> **Source dataset:** Telco_customer_churn.xlsx — 7,043 customers, 1,869 churners
> (26.54% base rate)

## What changed from version 1

Version 1 contained 57 documents, of which **20 were empty**: each `EXPLORATORY-*`
entry stated that associations "were calculated" and then reported none. Those 20
are now `ASSOC-*` documents containing the actual measured subgroup shares against
the all-churner baseline. Two new document types were added: the derivation of the
launch effect estimates (`DELTA-*`), and the template for outcome evidence
(`OUTCOME-TEMPLATE`) which is the document class that will grow over time.

## What changed in version 4 — the retrieval fix

Versions 1–3 were organised entirely around `Churn Reason`. That field is a
post-exit survey answer: **it does not exist for a live customer.** So 39 of the 63
documents could never be retrieved for someone we are about to call, and putting
them in a prompt anyway invites the narration layer to guess a motive.

We tested the bridge we had been relying on — that a customer's observable levers
predict which reason theme they would cite. Measured over all 1,869 churners,
across all 63 (lever, theme) pairs, **every lift is noise**: maximum 1.13, minimum
0.84, and not one pair exceeds 1.15 with adequate support. The bridge does not
exist. It was judgment, and the data does not support it.

The same levers separate churn itself by **2.17x to 6.32x**. So version 4 adds a
`LEVER-*` document per lever, computed from observable account fields only, with no
reason involved at any step. Those, plus the offer's `DELTA-*` derivation and the
three `POLICY-*` guardrails, are the **only** documents the narration layer
retrieves. Every reason-keyed document remains here as offline market intelligence
and is marked `historical_cohort` in `artifacts/evidence_ids.json`.

## How to read this knowledge base — read this first

Three things are true of almost every association in here, and the narration layer
must not overstate them:

1. **The reason themes are barely separable on the available features.** Median
   monthly charges span **$5.00** across all seven themes; median tenure spans
   **3 months**. A multiclass model trained to predict the theme scores 0.348
   accuracy against 0.331 for guessing the largest class. There is essentially no
   signal.
2. **Therefore the associations below are weak by construction.** The largest
   single deviation from baseline anywhere in this file is about 22 percentage
   points, and it occurs on a subgroup of 20 customers. Most are under 10 points.
3. **None of it is causal, and none of it is a live churn reason.** `Churn Reason`
   is a post-exit survey field. It does not exist for a customer who has not left.
   It is used HERE, offline, to build aggregate market intelligence — never as a
   feature and never as a claim about a live customer.

## Evidence governance

- **`dataset_fact`** — directly computed from the supplied dataset. Reliable as a
  description of history.
- **`dataset_association`** — measured subgroup share vs baseline. Exploratory,
  possibly confounded, never a recommendation trigger on its own.
- **`external_research_evidence`** — domain evidence from cited literature.
- **`candidate_retention_consideration`** — a possible action. Not a company
  policy and not a proven treatment effect.
- **`effect_derivation`** — how a launch Δ estimate was arrived at, and how much
  of the observed gap it assumes is causal.
- **`system_policy`** — rules governing prediction, retrieval and generation.
- **`outcome_evidence`** — a MEASURED treatment effect from the control-group
  experiment. **This class is empty today** and is the only class that will ever
  justify a causal claim.

## Important guardrails

- `Churn Reason` is post-outcome information and must **never** be a live model
  feature. Its nullity alone reproduces the label with accuracy 1.000.
- SHAP / top model features explain **model behaviour**, not customer motive.
  Label them `model_attribution`, never `reason`.
- Historical associations must not be presented as causal relationships.
- The system must not invent company policies, discount amounts, offer IDs, or
  guaranteed retention outcomes. Every `offer_id` and `evidence_id` an LLM emits
  is validated against its registry and rejected if unknown.
- `delta_prior` values are **assumptions**, not measurements. Any statement of
  expected value must carry its uncertainty range.
- If an unseen situation occurs, retrieve semantically related validated concepts.
  Do not fabricate historical evidence.
- Involuntary churn (Moved, Deceased — 59 historical cases) appears in this
  historical data but **cannot be detected for a live customer**. The only field
  that records it is `Churn Reason`, which is post-outcome and quarantined. The
  policy rule that used to route these customers out was removed in v4 because it
  was reading that quarantined column. Re-introducing the route requires an
  account-status feed from the billing system, not a model.

### OUTCOME-TEMPLATE

This is the template for the only document class that can ever support a causal

```
### OUTCOME-<n>
- Document type: outcome_evidence
- Segment: MONTH_TO_MONTH + NEW_CUSTOMER
- Offer: OFF-CONTRACT-1Y
- Evidence type: outcome_evidence
- Evidence strength: measured
- n_treated: 412   retained 78.4%
- n_control:  46   retained 59.5%

- horizon_days: 90
- supersedes: DELTA-<n> (assumption, delta_prior 0.12)

Among customers with segment X who were offered Y, 90-day retention was A%
against B% for the randomised control group, a measured lift of C points.
This REPLACES the launch assumption of D.
```

Until documents of this class exist, the narration layer must describe every
expected-value figure as resting on an assumption.

### POLICY-001

- **Document type:** `system_policy`
- **Theme:** live_prediction
- **Evidence type:** `system_policy`
- **Source IDs:** ['SYSTEM_DESIGN']

`Churn Reason` is post-outcome information and must not be an input to the live
churn model. SHAP / top contributing features explain model behaviour; they do not
reveal a customer's motive. The decision of which offer to make is taken by a
deterministic expected-value calculation and a versioned policy engine, before any
language model is invoked. No language model may select, price, or alter an offer.

### POLICY-002

- **Document type:** `retrieval_policy`
- **Theme:** context_assembly
- **Evidence type:** `system_policy`
- **Source IDs:** ['SYSTEM_DESIGN']

This knowledge base is loaded **in full into the system prompt** and cached. It is
not retrieved by vector search. At its current size that makes retrieval an exact
dictionary lookup on `theme`, which cannot mis-retrieve and cannot omit a
guardrail.

Switch conditions, in order:

- **under ~25,000 tokens** — full context, dictionary lookup by theme (current state)
- **25,000 to ~100,000 tokens** — full context plus theme filtering before assembly
- **over ~100,000 tokens** — hybrid retrieval (BM25 + dense) with hard metadata
  filters on `document_type`, `theme` and `evidence_type`

Guardrails are NEVER retrieved. They live in the system prompt unconditionally, so
that no retrieval failure can drop a safety rule. Growth will come mainly from
`outcome_evidence` documents, one per segment-offer pair.

### POLICY-003

- **Document type:** `recommendation_policy`
- **Theme:** evidence_governance
- **Evidence type:** `system_policy`
- **Source IDs:** ['SYSTEM_DESIGN']

Generated text may present candidate retention considerations but must not invent

and retried, then to fall back to a deterministic template.
